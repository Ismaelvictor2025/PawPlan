# PawPlan — App Store App Preview Video Storyboard (30 Seconds)

## Production Specifications
- **Resolution**: 1080 x 1920 (9:16) or 886 x 1920 (iPhone 16 Pro Max / 17 Pro Max)
- **Duration**: Exactly 30 seconds
- **Audio Track**: Ambient upbeat lo-fi acoustic guitar with crisp SFX
- **Color Grading**: True Electric Ocean dark theme with vibrant turquoise/cyan accents

---

## Storyboard Timeline

### Scene 1 (0:00 - 0:05) — "Every Paw Deserves a Plan"
- **Visual**: App launches with the dynamic Electric Ocean splash card. Ripple animation around glowing paw glyph. Transitions seamlessly into the Today Dashboard showing "Good morning, Archie!".
- **On-Screen Text**: "DOG CARE, SIMPLIFIED."
- **Voiceover**: "Bringing a dog home changes your life. Keeping up with their care shouldn't feel overwhelming."
- **SFX**: Gentle chime, soft haptic pop.

### Scene 2 (0:05 - 0:11) — "One-Tap Daily Routines"
- **Visual**: User taps "+ Walk" and marks "Morning River Walk" complete. Daily completion ring animates to 100% with glowing cyan confetti micro-animation.
- **On-Screen Text**: "SMART RECURRING ROUTINES"
- **Voiceover**: "Track walks, feeding, and medications with automatic daily recurrence and zero stress."
- **SFX**: Crisp haptic completion click.

### Scene 3 (0:11 - 0:17) — "Sydney Off-Leash Discovery"
- **Visual**: Transition to Explore tab. Map opens over Sydney Harbour. Filter by "Dog-Friendly Beach" highlights Sirius Cove and Rose Bay Beach. Quick card expansion shows off-leash hours and amenities.
- **On-Screen Text**: "CURATED SYDNEY DOG SPOTS"
- **Voiceover**: "Find Sydney's best off-leash beaches, fenced parks, and dog-friendly cafes nearby."
- **SFX**: Smooth swipe whoosh.

### Scene 4 (0:17 - 0:23) — "Health, Weights & Emergency Hub"
- **Visual**: Switch to Health tab. Shows Cavoodle growth trajectory chart and vaccine certificates. Quick swipe to More tab reveals the 24/7 Emergency Vet Hub (SASH and UVTHS) with one-tap emergency calling.
- **On-Screen Text**: "MEDICAL VAULT & 24/7 EMERGENCY READY"
- **Voiceover**: "Stay ahead of vaccinations, weight trends, and 24/7 emergency veterinary care when every second counts."
- **SFX**: Subtle heartbeat transition.

### Scene 5 (0:23 - 0:30) — "Puppy Academy & Call to Action"
- **Visual**: Preview of 12-week Puppy Academy development milestones. Camera zooms out to show Archie and Bella profile switcher. Closing logo lockup with Electric Ocean turquoise glow.
- **On-Screen Text**:
  - "PAWPLAN"
  - "Happy paws. Every day."
  - "Free on the App Store"
- **Voiceover**: "PawPlan. Happy paws, every day. Download today on iOS."
- **SFX**: Melodic closing chord.
