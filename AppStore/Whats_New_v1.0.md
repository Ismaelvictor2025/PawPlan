# PawPlan — Version 1.0.0 Release Notes ("What's New")

## Welcome to PawPlan: Happy paws. Every day.

We are thrilled to introduce **PawPlan 1.0.0**, the native iOS care companion built specifically for first-time dog parents across Sydney, Australia!

### What's Included in Version 1.0.0:
- **Electric Ocean Design:** A bespoke, dark-mode native visual experience crafted with deep oceanic navy and vibrant electric turquoise accents.
- **Smart Routine Planning:** Effortless scheduling for walks, feeding, grooming, and medications with automatic daily/weekly recurrence.
- **Today Dashboard:** High-level summary of upcoming activities, quick-action logging, and daily care consistency scores.
- **Health & Weight Vault:** Record veterinary appointments, log vaccination certificates, and track growth trajectories over time.
- **Sydney Pet Places:** Curated directory of off-leash dog beaches, fenced parks, dog-friendly cafes, and 24/7 emergency veterinary clinics with direct call and navigation links.
- **Puppy Academy:** A structured 12-week development roadmap covering essential puppy milestones, socialization windows, and foundation training tips.
- **Expense & Budget Tracker:** Organize pet expenses by category, set monthly limits, and export itemized CSV reports for insurance claims.
- **NSW Pet Regulations:** Quick-reference guide to the NSW Companion Animals Act, council lifetime registration, microchip rules, and tick prevention seasons.
- **Document Vault:** Securely store photos of council registration tags, desexing certificates, and insurance policies on your device.
- **Multi-Dog Profiles:** Easily manage routines and records for multiple furry family members.
- **Privacy-First Architecture:** 100% on-device storage with Apple Core Data. No accounts, no advertising, and zero data tracking.

We love hearing your feedback! Send your suggestions and puppy photos to hello@pawplan.app.
