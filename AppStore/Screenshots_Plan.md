# PawPlan — App Store Screenshots Plan & Visual Narrative

## 1. Device Targets
- **6.9" Super Retina XDR** (iPhone 16 Pro Max / 17 Pro Max): 1320 x 2868 px portrait
- **6.3" Super Retina XDR** (iPhone 16 Pro / 17 Pro): 1206 x 2622 px portrait
- **Format**: High-resolution 24-bit PNG, RGB color profile, uncompressed.

## 3. Visual Styling Standards
- **Background**: Electric Ocean Deep Navy (`#080F20`).
- **Typography**: San Francisco Pro Display Heavy for headlines, Semibold for subheads.
- **Accents**: Electric Turquoise (`#15D9D2`) highlights with subtle glow shadows.
- **Device Framing**: Borderless floating perspective with subtle 12% ambient drop shadow.
