# PawPlan — Support & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. General Questions

### What is PawPlan?
PawPlan is a native iOS application designed specifically to empower first-time dog parents in Sydney, Australia. It combines daily routine management, medical history tracking, Sydney off-leash discovery, puppy development milestones, and emergency vet routing into one private, beautifully designed experience.

### Does PawPlan require an internet connection or account?
No. PawPlan operates completely offline. There are no logins, passwords, or mandatory cloud accounts. All your dog's photos, routines, vaccination histories, and expenses are stored locally on your device.

### Can I track multiple dogs?
Yes! Tap the dog profile button in the top right corner of the Today screen to switch between pets or add new profiles. Each dog has isolated routines, medical logs, expenses, and growth records.

## 2. Daily Care & Routines

### How does recurring task scheduling work?
When creating or editing a care task (like a daily morning walk or bi-weekly grooming session), you can choose a recurrence rule:
- **Daily:** Automatically reschedules for tomorrow at the same time once marked complete.
- **Weekdays (Mon–Fri):** Advances to the next weekday upon completion.
- **Weekends (Sat–Sun):** Advances to the next weekend day.
- **Weekly / Bi-Weekly / Monthly:** Advances to the next cycle.
- **One-time:** Remains marked complete without rescheduling.

### Why didn't I receive a notification?
Ensure notifications are enabled for PawPlan in your iPhone's **Settings > PawPlan > Notifications**. Local notifications are scheduled directly on your device without using external push servers.

## 3. Sydney Dog Features & Regulations

### Which Sydney dog beaches and parks are included?
PawPlan features hand-curated spots across the Sydney basin, including Sirius Cove Reserve (Mosman), Rose Bay Foreshore Beach, Sydney Park (Alexandria), Rowland Reserve (Bayview), Marks Park (Tamarama), and Centennial Parklands. Each location includes verified off-leash hours, water station notes, and fence status.

### What are the NSW registration requirements for dogs?
In New South Wales:
1. **Microchipping:** Mandatory by 12 weeks of age (or prior to sale/transfer).
2. **NSW Pet Registry:** Lifetime registration with your local council is mandatory by 6 months of age. Substantial discounts apply if your dog is desexed before registration.
3. **Leash Laws:** Dogs must be kept on a physical leash in all public spaces unless inside designated off-leash council areas.

### What should I do in a veterinary emergency?
Navigate to the **More** tab and select **Emergency Care Hub**. PawPlan provides one-tap direct calling and directions to Sydney's primary 24-hour emergency hospitals:
- **SASH North Ryde / Alexandria / Western Sydney** (Small Animal Specialist Hospital)
- **UVTHS Camperdown** (University Veterinary Teaching Hospital Sydney)
- **ARH Homebush** (Animal Referral Hospital)

## 4. Data & Backups

### How do I back up my pet data?
Because PawPlan uses Apple Core Data on your device, all data is automatically backed up during your standard encrypted iCloud or Mac device backups.

### Can I export my pet expenses for tax or insurance claims?
Yes. Go to **More > Pet Care Expenses & Budget** and tap **Export CSV**. PawPlan will generate an itemized CSV file formatted for Excel, Numbers, or insurance reimbursement submission.
